About Us

Where American Education Meets Pakistani Heritage

Founded in 1956, Lahore American School stands as Pakistan's premier institution for American curriculum education. Located on the historic Canal Road, our campus combines state-of-the-art facilities with a rich legacy of academic excellence.

We are accredited by the Middle States Association of Colleges and Schools (MSA) and offer a comprehensive program from Early Childhood through Grade 12, including Advanced Placement courses for college-bound students.
